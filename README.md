# Tesla vs. Subaru: A Model Comparison

This document provides a comparison between popular Tesla models (Model 3 and Model Y) and similarly sized or purposed models from Subaru.

## Tesla Model 3 vs. Subaru Counterparts

The Tesla Model 3 is a mid-size all-electric sedan known for its performance, technology, and range. Its Subaru counterparts are primarily traditional internal combustion engine (ICE) sedans.

### Tesla Model 3

*   **Type:** All-Electric Mid-Size Sedan
*   **Dimensions (approx. post-2023 facelift):** 185.8" L x 72.8" W x 56.7" H
*   **Powertrain Options:** Rear-Wheel Drive (RWD) or Dual-Motor All-Wheel Drive (AWD)
*   **Battery/Range (approx. EPA est.):** 57.5 kWh to 82 kWh; 272 mi (RWD) to 341 mi (LR AWD)
*   **Key Highlights:** Performance variants (0-60 mph as low as 3.1s), Autopilot advanced driver-assist, large central touchscreen interface, access to Tesla Supercharger network.

### Subaru Legacy

*   **Type:** ICE Mid-Size Sedan (7th Gen: 2020-2025)
*   **Dimensions (approx. 7th Gen):** ~190.6" L x 72.4" W x 59.1" H
*   **Powertrain Options (7th Gen):
    *   2.5L Flat-Four (approx. 182 hp)
    *   2.4L Turbocharged Flat-Four (approx. 260 hp)
*   **Transmission:** Lineartronic CVT
*   **Key Highlights:** Standard Symmetrical All-Wheel Drive, EyeSight Driver Assist Technology, comfortable and spacious interior.

### Subaru WRX

*   **Type:** ICE Sport Compact Sedan (2nd Gen VB: 2022-present)
*   **Dimensions (approx. 2nd Gen VB):** ~183.8" L x 71.9" W x 57.8" H
*   **Powertrain (2nd Gen VB):** 2.4L Turbocharged Flat-Four (approx. 271 hp)
*   **Transmission:** 6-speed Manual or Subaru Performance Transmission (SPT - CVT)
*   **Key Highlights:** Rally-inspired performance and handling, advanced Symmetrical All-Wheel Drive, driver-focused cockpit.

## Tesla Model Y vs. Subaru Counterparts

The Tesla Model Y is a compact all-electric crossover SUV offering utility, performance, and EV technology. Subaru offers several ICE and one EV crossover/SUV model in similar segments.

### Tesla Model Y

*   **Type:** All-Electric Compact Crossover SUV
*   **Dimensions (approx.):** 187" L x 75.6" W x 63.9" H
*   **Powertrain Options:** Dual-Motor All-Wheel Drive (AWD) is most common.
*   **Battery/Range (approx. EPA est.):** 60 kWh to 81 kWh; ~260-330 mi (RWD/LR AWD variants)
*   **Key Highlights:** Optional 7-seat configuration, panoramic glass roof, Autopilot, large central touchscreen, good cargo space (including front trunk), access to Tesla Supercharger network.

### Subaru Forester

*   **Type:** ICE Compact Crossover SUV (5th Gen SK: 2019-2024; 6th Gen SL: 2025-)
*   **Dimensions (approx. 5th Gen):** ~182.1" L x 71.5" W x 68.1" H (varies by trim)
*   **Powertrain (North America, 5th/6th Gen):** 2.5L Flat-Four (approx. 180-182 hp)
*   **Powertrain (Other markets):** e-Boxer Hybrid options available.
*   **Transmission:** Lineartronic CVT
*   **Key Highlights:** Standard Symmetrical AWD, EyeSight Driver Assist, X-MODE for enhanced off-road capability, spacious and practical interior, excellent visibility. Wilderness trim offers increased ground clearance and ruggedness.

### Subaru Outback

*   **Type:** ICE Mid-Size Crossover/Wagon (6th Gen BT: 2020-2025; 7th Gen BU: 2026-)
*   **Dimensions (approx. 6th Gen):** ~191.3" L x 73" W x 66.1" H (varies by trim)
*   **Powertrain Options (North America, 6th Gen):
    *   2.5L Flat-Four (approx. 182 hp)
    *   2.4L Turbocharged Flat-Four (XT models, approx. 260 hp)
*   **Transmission:** Lineartronic CVT
*   **Key Highlights:** Standard Symmetrical AWD, EyeSight, ample cargo space, comfortable ride, available Wilderness trim with higher ground clearance (9.5"), more rugged features, and turbocharged engine. 7th Gen (2026) leans more into SUV styling.

### Subaru Solterra

*   **Type:** All-Electric Compact Crossover SUV
*   **Dimensions (approx.):** ~184.6" L x 73.2" W x 65.0" H
*   **Powertrain:** Dual-Motor All-Wheel Drive (AWD) standard (approx. 215 hp total)
*   **Battery/Range (approx. EPA est.):** ~72.8 kWh battery; ~222-228 mi range.
*   **Key Highlights:** Standard Symmetrical AWD with X-MODE, co-developed with Toyota (shares platform with bZ4X), EyeSight (Toyota Safety Sense based), EV-specific features. Facelift expected to improve range/charging.

## General Observations

*   **Powertrain:** The most significant difference is Tesla's all-electric lineup versus Subaru's predominantly ICE lineup (with the Solterra EV as an exception and emerging hybrid options for Forester in some markets).
*   **Technology & Interior:** Tesla models generally emphasize cutting-edge technology, large central touchscreens, and autonomous driving features with a minimalist interior. Subaru models focus on safety technology like EyeSight and practical infotainment, with a more traditional and functional interior layout.
*   **All-Wheel Drive:** Subaru is renowned for its standard Symmetrical All-Wheel Drive system across its lineup, emphasizing capability in various weather and light off-road conditions. Tesla offers AWD as an option or standard on higher trims, often focused on enhancing performance and traction.
*   **Design Philosophy:** Tesla often opts for minimalist, aerodynamic, and futuristic designs. Subaru tends towards more utilitarian and rugged aesthetics, particularly with models like the Forester and Outback, often featuring more protective body cladding.
*   **Infrastructure & Range:** Tesla benefits from its proprietary Supercharger network for convenient long-distance EV travel. Subaru's ICE models rely on traditional gas stations. The Solterra uses general EV charging infrastructure (CCS), and its range is generally less than comparable Tesla models.
*   **Performance Focus:** While Tesla offers high-performance variants across its range, Subaru's performance focus is traditionally centered on the WRX (for sedans) and specific turbocharged Outback/Forester XT models, with a different character (rally-inspired vs. straight-line EV acceleration).
